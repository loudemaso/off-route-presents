The sound of a gate closing

A field recording finds a second life in a Silt Index rhythm.

Fri, Nov 13, 2026

On its own, the recording is a short metallic scrape followed by a clunk. Silt Index trims the clunk to a fraction of a second, tunes it down and places it between two kicks. The scrape gets a longer tail and becomes a faint sweep behind the snare.

Both sounds surface in the Low Water Mark set. Listen first for the short hit, then for its rougher twin hanging in the echo. What started as a single gesture now gives the track both its pulse and its sense of distance.
