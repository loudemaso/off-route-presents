Drawing Fern Frequency

A loudspeaker, a fern and the pink ink that holds the flyer together.

Fri, Nov 6, 2026

The Fern Frequency poster starts with a loudspeaker cone. Follow its rim and the shape reads as a circle. Follow the frond rising from it and the same cone starts to look like a planter. That second reading gave the winter closer its image.

The lineup sits apart from the illustration so both names survive a quick glance on a phone. Pink carries the eye through the dark green, from the title to the details. The event page pairs the finished artwork with the full bill and door time.
