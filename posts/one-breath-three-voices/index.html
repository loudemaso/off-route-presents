One breath, three voices

Inside the vocal loop at the center of Velour Antenna’s live set.

Mon, Nov 16, 2026

Watch the first pass through a Velour Antenna chorus and the microphone does most of the work. A held vowel supplies the note. A second pass, sung a little lower, turns it into a chord. The third voice arrives late enough to sound like an answer from the back of the room.

For Velvet Current, the arrangement leaves the first verse nearly bare. That gives the ear a reference point when the sampler fills up. By the time the kick returns, the chorus is carrying its own bass and percussion, all borrowed from the same breath.
