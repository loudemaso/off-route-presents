Mica Relay

Broken beat / house

Biography

Mica Relay listens for the little hitch in a drum loop. Behind two turntables, she threads broken beat into warm, rolling house, letting an off-center snare hang for a bar longer than expected. Her favorite transition starts with two records that seem to disagree and ends with the whole floor stepping to both.
