Juniper Static

Acid disco / leftfield house

Biography

Juniper Static keeps a pencil in the record bag to mark the bar where a sweet disco cut turns strange. Her sets follow those turns, jumping from elastic bass guitar to squelchy acid lines without losing the dancers in the front row. The record she saves for last usually has the loosest drums.
